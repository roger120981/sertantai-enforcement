## UK Gambling Commission Enforcement

The Gambling Commission is the UK's independent gambling regulator. Established under the Gambling Act 2005, it replaced the Gaming Board for Great Britain in 2007. It regulates almost all forms of gambling in the UK, including betting, lotteries, gaming machines, and online platforms.(no content)The Commission investigates misconduct and enforces compliance. Operators face compliance audits through documents, interviews, and covert tests. Outcomes may include warnings or advice, additional licence conditions, suspension or revocation of licences, and financial penalties.(no content)The Commission has issued some of the heaviest fines in UK gambling, often for failures in money laundering prevention or protecting vulnerable users. Fines both punish offenders and deter the wider industry, and funds raised often go toward problem gambling treatment and prevention.
