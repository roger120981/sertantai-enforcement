## Overview of MHRA

The Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency regulates medicines, medical devices and blood components for transfusion in the UK.(no content)MHRA is an executive agency, sponsored by the Department of Health and Social Care.

## Enforcement Powers and Activities

Powers available to MHRA enforcement officers include investigation and prosecution, entry to commercial premises or private dwellings, and the inspection and seizure of illegal products.(no content)The MHRA investigates internet sales and potential counterfeiting of medicines, and prosecutes where necessary.

### Criminal Enforcement Unit (CEU)

The Criminal Enforcement Unit (CEU) is the MHRA's in-house law enforcement function, leading the Agency's response to medicines crime. Its strategic mission is to protect the public, maintain confidence in regulation and uphold the rule of law by preventing offending where it can, disrupting offending where it cannot, and bringing offenders to justice where it should.(no content)It uses the full range of its powers and capabilities, including intelligence analysis, online disruption, covert techniques, and asset recovery to tackle criminal threats to the UK public, working closely with the police and law enforcement agencies in the UK and overseas.

## Recent Enforcement Actions

### 2023 Seizures
The MHRA, working with law enforcement partners, seized more than 15.5 million doses of illegally traded medicines with a street value of more than £30 million during 2023.(no content)Last year, six MHRA investigations resulted in significant custodial sentences for many of those convicted. These prosecutions followed detailed investigations, the seizure of millions of doses of medicines.

### Operation Lamborghini (November 2025)
The investigation, led by the MHRA's Criminal Enforcement Unit (CEU) and known as Operation Lamborghini, uncovered an organised criminal network operating a series of websites to illegally supply unlicensed medicines. Everton Reynolds, Paul Billingham, and Junior Ranger were convicted by a jury of conspiracy to supply controlled drugs (Class B and C), and conspiracies to supply unauthorised medicines, prescription only medicines, following a 6-week trial at Wolverhampton Crown Court.

### Weight Loss Medicine Seizure (October 2025)
Officers from the Criminal Enforcement Unit (CEU) of the MHRA have dismantled a major illicit manufacturing facility, making and distributing unlicensed weight-loss jabs, during a raid on a warehouse in Northampton. This is the first illicit production facility for weight loss medicine discovered in the UK and is believed to be the largest single seizure of trafficked weight loss medicines ever recorded by a law enforcement agency worldwide.

## Reporting Illegal Activity

You can notify the MHRA about a suspicious online seller using the Criminal Enforcement Unit reporting service. For queries on potential illegal dealings with medicines, email thebureau@mhra.gov.uk.(no content)Anyone who suspects illegal trading in medicines or believes a product may be counterfeit can report it via the Yellow Card scheme.
