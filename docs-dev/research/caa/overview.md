## Civil Aviation Authority (CAA)

The main purpose of the Civil Aviation Authority's (CAA's) enforcement role is to protect consumers and the public by encouraging compliance with rules applicable to civil aviation and to deter non-compliance.(no content)As outlined in their Regulatory Enforcement Policy, some enforcement actions are published to provide a transparent mechanism by which consumers and the public can be informed about the actions taken, and to provide a wider deterrent for non-compliance.(no content)The CAA has civil powers to take enforcement action in relation to a range of passenger rights legislation and general consumer law.(no content)The CAA is the UK's aviation and aerospace regulator, and its licensing, certification and approvals are wide-ranging, from approving orbital space flight launches to licensing over 40,000 UK pilots to permitting half a million drone users to operate annually.
