## Care Quality Commission (CQC)

The Care Quality Commission (CQC) regulates adult social care services in England, including care homes, nursing homes, and home care. Established in 2009 under the Health and Social Care Act 2008, the CQC conducts registrations, inspections, and enforcement actions.(no content)Some of the regulations have offences attached, and as part of their enforcement action, CQC is able to bring prosecutions if these regulations are breached.(no content)The CQC has a wide range of civil and criminal enforcement powers, including the cancellation of registration. The CQC can utilise these powers against registered providers and managers within the health and adult social care sectors who are in breach of the fundamental standards, a condition of their registration, or any associated legislation.(no content)The CQC has been increasingly 'baring its teeth' in terms of enforcement, with action to address 'Inadequate' services through the Special Measures regime and prosecutions of regulatory breaches, with unlimited maximum fines becoming more commonplace.
