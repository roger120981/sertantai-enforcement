## Food Standards Agency (FSA)

The Food Law Code of Practice sets out how local authorities and other enforcement bodies should carry out official food law controls in England, dictating how inspections, enforcement action and prosecutions are approached.(no content)It is the responsibility of every local authority to enforce the Food Safety Act 1990 with regard to restaurants, cafes and any other establishments that make food for human consumption. All restaurants are inspected to ensure their food hygiene and safety practices are in accordance with industry-wide guidelines.

Recent FSA enforcement examples include:
- A West Yorkshire abattoir was ordered to pay more than £60,000 in fines and court costs after pleading guilty to obstructing Food Standards Agency officers from carrying out their regulatory hygiene inspection duties.
- An abattoir in Greater Manchester was ordered to pay substantial fines after being convicted of eleven food safety charges.(no content)The consequences of being prosecuted for food hygiene or safety offences can be very serious, and taking expert legal advice as soon as possible can make a significant difference to the final outcome.
