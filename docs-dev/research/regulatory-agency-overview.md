## Recommended Next Additions (Priority Order)

### 1. SEPA (Scottish Environment Protection Agency) - **HIGH**
- **Why:** Completes UK environmental coverage alongside EA/NRW
- **Data:** Prosecutions, enforcement notices, civil penalties
- **URL:** https://www.sepa.org.uk/regulations/enforcement/
- **Access:** Public register available

### 2. NRW (Natural Resources Wales) - **HIGH**  
- **Why:** Completes UK environmental coverage
- **Data:** Similar to EA - prosecutions, notices
- **URL:** https://naturalresources.wales/guidance-and-advice/environmental-topics/
- **Access:** Public register

### 3. Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) - **MEDIUM**
- **Why:** Critical safety regulator, publishes prosecutions
- **Data:** Aviation safety prosecutions, enforcement actions
- **URL:** https://www.caa.co.uk/our-work/about-us/enforcement/
- **Access:** HTML pages

### 4. Care Quality Commission (CQC) - **MEDIUM**
- **Why:** Major health/social care regulator
- **Data:** Prosecutions, warning notices, registration cancellations
- **URL:** https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/enforcement
- **Access:** Searchable database

### 5. Gambling Commission - **MEDIUM**
- **Why:** Growing regulatory focus, transparent enforcement
- **Data:** Licence revocations, fines, regulatory settlements
- **URL:** https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register
- **Access:** Public register with search

### 6. Drinking Water Inspectorate (DWI) - **MEDIUM**
- **Why:** Water safety is high public interest
- **Data:** Enforcement undertakings, prosecutions
- **URL:** https://www.dwi.gov.uk/what-we-do/enforcement/
- **Access:** Published reports

### 7. Food Standards Agency (FSA) - **LOWER**
- **Why:** Enforcement decentralized to local authorities
- **Data:** LA prosecutions compiled annually
- **URL:** https://www.food.gov.uk/topic/enforcement
- **Access:** Aggregated reports, not granular

### 8. ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) - **LOWER**
- **Why:** Data protection focus, not traditional safety
- **Data:** Monetary penalties, enforcement notices, reprimands
- **URL:** https://ico.org.uk/action-weve-taken/
- **Access:** Public register

## Summary Recommendation

To achieve **comprehensive UK safety enforcement coverage**, I recommend adding in this order:

1. **SEPA** + **NRW** - Complete environmental coverage across UK nations
2. **CAA** - Critical transport safety (aviation complements ORR rail, MCA maritime)
3. **CQC** - Health and social care sector
4. **Gambling Commission** - Well-structured public data
5. **DWI** - Water safety (high public interest)

This would give coverage across: workplace, environment (all UK), fire, maritime, rail, aviation, products, and health/social care - representing the major safety-critical regulatory domains.
