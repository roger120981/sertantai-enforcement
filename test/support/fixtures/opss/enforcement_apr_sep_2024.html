The Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS), the UK's product regulator is part of the Department for Business and Trade.

Environmental Protection – August 2024

Business: CVS ENERGY LTD t/a Clearview Stoves

Action taken:

Compliance Notice dated 21 August 2024, served under Regulation 13(b) and Schedule 5, para 6(1)(a) of the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010.

Products:

  • Pioneer 400 Stove
  • Pioneer 400P Stove
  • Solution 400 Stove

Breached regulation details:

Regulation 14(1) of the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010.

Detail:

The manufacturer failed to make all relevant technical information and documentation including Declarations of Conformity for the above products, available for inspection by OPSS.

Product Safety – April 2024

Business: LVLIANGSHILISHIQUHAITAOBAIHUOSHANGHANG t/a haitao-shop (online seller, based in China)

Action taken:

Recall Notice dated 15 April 2024, served under Regulation 15(1) of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005, by virtue of Regulation 53(2)(d) of the Toy (Safety) Regulations 2011.

Product:

Magnetic Building Blocks Educational Toys Set

Detail:

The product presents a serious risk due to the presence of small parts with high magnetic flux. The magnets can be swallowed and cause serious internal injuries.

The online seller is required to recall the product from end users. The online marketplace (AliExpress) removed the product listing.

Business: CHANGSHA YUNLE CHILDREN'S PRODUCTS CO LTD t/a shop1100009001 store (online seller, based in China)

Action taken:

Withdrawal Notice dated 22 April 2024, served under Regulation 14(1) of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

Product:

Children's Balance Bike with removable pedals

Detail:

The product fails to meet the safety requirements set out in BS EN 71-1:2014+A1:2018 due to sharp edges and small parts that could detach.

The online marketplace (Amazon) removed the product listing.

Construction Products – June 2024

Business: QUALITY GLASS SOLUTIONS LTD

Action taken:

Stop Notice dated 18 June 2024, served under Regulation 11(1) of the Construction Products Regulations 2013.

Products:

Laminated safety glass panels for use in balustrades and structural glazing applications.

Breached regulation details:

Regulation 4(1)(b) of the Construction Products Regulations 2013.

Detail:

The manufacturer was unable to provide a valid CE marking or UK Conformity Assessed marking for the products. Production was halted pending compliance demonstration.

Timber – September 2024

Business: TROPICAL HARDWOODS IMPORT LTD

Action taken:

Prosecution on 5 September 2024 (sentenced).

The company pleaded guilty at Westminster Magistrates' Court on 12 July 2024 to two offences under the Timber and Timber Products (Placing on the Market) Regulations 2013.

The court imposed a fine of £85,000 and ordered costs of £12,500.

Products:

Illegally harvested rosewood imported from Brazil without appropriate documentation.

Breached regulation details:

Regulations 4(a) and 4(b) of the Timber and Timber Products (Placing on the Market) Regulations 2013.

Detail:

The operator placed timber on the UK market without establishing a due diligence system and failed to demonstrate negligible risk of illegal harvesting for imports of CITES-listed rosewood species.
