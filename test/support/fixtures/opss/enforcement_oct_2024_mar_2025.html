The Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS), the UK's product regulator is part of the Department for Business and Trade, and exercises the powers of the Secretary of State in relation to a range of regulations.

Construction Products – October 2024

Business: SUPER TOUGHENED GLASS LTD

Action taken:

Prohibition Notice dated 24 October 2024, served with effect from 4 November 2024 under Regulation 11(1) of the Construction Products Regulations 2013, as applicable in England, Wales and Scotland.

Products:

Any products placed on the market as heat-soaked thermally toughened soda lime silicate safety glass irrespective of size, thickness and shape, for use in buildings, that cannot be determined to meet the specifications of designated standard BS EN 14179-2, manufactured on-site by Super Toughened Glass Ltd trading as Super Tuff at Glass House Unit 2, 5-9 Willenfield Road, Park Royal, London NW10 7BQ.

Breached regulation details:

Regulation 4(1)(a) of the Construction Products Regulations 2013.

Detail:

The Notice prohibits the supply of the above products, as upon request by OPSS, the manufacturer was unable to provide a declaration of performance.

The manufacturer subsequently provided the required documentation. The Prohibition Notice was revoked on 13 January 2025.

Environmental Protection - October 2024

Business: CVS Energy Ltd t/a Clearview Stoves

Action taken:

Stop Notice dated 21 October 2024, served under Regulation 13(b) and Schedule 5, para 13(1)(a) of the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010.

Products:

  • Pioneer 400 Stove
  • Pioneer 400P Stove
  • Solution 400 Stove
  • Vision Inset Deep Stove
  • Pioneer Oven Stove with Oven & Hotplate
  • Vision 500 Stove with Hotplate
  • Solution 500 Stove
  • Solution 500SB Stove with integrated Boiler
  • Clearview 650 Stove with Hotplate
  • Clearview 750 Stove with Hotplate

Breached regulation details:

Regulation 14(1) of the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010.

Detail:

The manufacturer failed to make all relevant technical information and documentation including Declarations of Conformity for the above products, available for inspection by OPSS.

Furthermore, the manufacturer failed to take action or respond to the previously served Compliance Notices (dated 21 August 2024). Manufacturer prohibited from placing listed products on to the market until a completion certificate is issued by OPSS.

Product Safety – November 2024

Business: SUZHOUYUEZHIFUKEJI CO., LTD t/a Joyful Inspiration (online seller, based in China)

Action taken:

Recall Notice dated 21 November 2024, served under Regulation 15(1) of the General Product Safety Regulations 2005, by virtue of Regulation 53(2)(d) of the Toy (Safety) Regulations 2011.

Product:

5 packs, hot selling water balloon reusable water balloon silicone water polo toy decompression silicone toy water balloon

Detail:

The product presents a serious risk of choking and injuries due to the presence of small parts. Some parts also have a magnetic flux over the allowable limit. Small, high-powered magnetic products can be easily swallowed and, when ingested, have the potential to cause serious internal injuries within the gastrointestinal tract.

The online seller is required to recall the product from end users. The online marketplace (Temu) removed the product listing.

Timber – November 2024

Business: SUNSEEKER INTERNATIONAL LIMITED

Action taken:

Prosecution on 22 November 2024 (sentenced).

The business pleaded guilty to the 3 offences outlined below, on 14 June 2023 at Poole Magistrates' Court. Following plea, the case was committed to Bournemouth Crown Court for confiscation proceedings under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 and for sentence.

The Crown Court imposed a fine in the amount of £240,000, a confiscation order for the amount of £66,950.64 and allowed costs in the sum of £51,619.96.

Products:

11 imports of timber products from the EU made between April 2021- July 2022:

  • Teak Decking-Trim Pieces-F/Bridge Stairs
  • Teak Door
  • Teak-Flybridge Support Piller Infill
  • Teak-Plank
  • Teak Decking
  • Teak Plank
  • Floor Wenge
  • Floor Wenge
  • Teak Plank
  • Floor Oak
  • Teak Plank

Breached regulation details:

Regulations 4(b), 4(c) and 4(d) of the Timber and Timber Products (Placing on the Market) Regulations 2013.

Detail:

Sunseeker International Limited is a manufacturer of yachts, it buys and imports timber from companies based in the EU. The company was acting as an Operator under Regulation (EU) No 995/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council ('the Timber Regulation') when it imported the 11 timber products from the EU into the United Kingdom.

The company failed to show negligible risk of illegal harvesting for the identified imports and was charged with the following three offences in respect of the imports of timber, which included Myanmar Teak, that were made between April 2021 - July 2022:

  1. Failed to exercise due diligence when placing timber products on the market, contrary to Regulation 4(b) of the Timber and Timber Products (Placing on the Market) Regulations 2013.
  2. Failed to maintain and evaluate the due diligence system which was established by Sunseeker International Limited or by a monitoring organisation when placing timber products on the market, contrary to Regulation 4(c) of the Timber and Timber Products (Placing on the Market) Regulations 2013.
  3. Failed to keep records in relation to the matters set out in Article 6(1)(a) of the Timber Regulation and in respect of risk mitigation procedures contrary to Regulation 4(e) of the Timber and Timber Products (Placing on the Market) Regulations 2013.

Timber – January 2025

Business: ACME TIMBER LTD

Action taken:

Compliance Notice dated 15 January 2025, served under Regulation 5(1) of the Timber and Timber Products (Placing on the Market) Regulations 2013.

Products:

Various hardwood flooring products imported from Southeast Asia.

Breached regulation details:

Regulation 4(a) of the Timber and Timber Products (Placing on the Market) Regulations 2013.

Detail:

The operator failed to establish and maintain a due diligence system when placing timber products on the market. A Compliance Notice was issued requiring the business to implement appropriate due diligence procedures within 60 days.
