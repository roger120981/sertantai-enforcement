                                    PROSECUTION SUMMARY

                        Office of Rail and Road v London Underground Limited
                                    and Balfour Beatty Rail Limited

Court:                           Westminster Magistrates Court

Sentencing date:                 17 November 2018

Defendants:                      London Underground Limited
                                 Balfour Beatty Rail Limited

Incident date:                   22 September 2014

Location:                        Whitechapel Station, London

Plea:                            Both defendants pleaded Guilty

Offences:

London Underground Limited - Section 2(1) of the Health and Safety at Work etc Act 1974

Balfour Beatty Rail Limited - Section 3(1) of the Health and Safety at Work etc Act 1974

Sentence:

London Underground Limited:
Fine: £100,000
Costs: £25,000

Balfour Beatty Rail Limited:
Fine: £80,000
Costs: £15,000

Total combined: £220,000

Details of the case:

On 22 September 2014, a contractor working for Balfour Beatty Rail Limited was struck by
a tube train at Whitechapel Station during night maintenance works. The worker suffered
serious injuries including a broken leg and head trauma.

The investigation found that London Underground had failed to ensure adequate safety
systems were in place for the protection of workers during engineering possessions.
Balfour Beatty Rail Limited failed to ensure their workers were not exposed to risks
from moving trains.

Both companies accepted responsibility and entered guilty pleas. The court noted that
both had cooperated fully with the investigation and had implemented improved safety
procedures.
