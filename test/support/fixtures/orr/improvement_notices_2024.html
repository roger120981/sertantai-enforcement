Improvement Notice issued to Great Western Society Limited

  • Issue date: 5 December 2024
  • Compliance date: 1 March 2025
  • Status: Complied
  • Public register ID: I/20241205/MDB/01

Great Western Society Limited has failed to ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, that its employees and persons other than its employees are not exposed to the risk of harm. It has not ensured that work equipment, including Thwaites Ltd Dumper Truck (serial number 14-99176), is maintained in an efficient state, in efficient working order and in good repair.

Improvement Notice issued to The Tramway Museum Society

  • Issue date: 11 November 2024
  • Compliance date: 9 December 2024
  • Status: Complied
  • Public register ID: I/ LS-20241111-1

The Tramway Museum Society have failed to make arrangements for the effective planning, monitoring and review of the preventative and protective measures that will, so far as is reasonably practicable, to ensure the safety of their employees and members of the public exposed to risk of injury.

Improvement Notice issued to Network Rail Infrastructure Limited

  • Issue date: 12 August 2024
  • Compliance date: 11 December 2024
  • Status: Complied
  • Public register ID: I/20240812/JGT

Network Rail Infrastructure Limited have failed to gain an authorisation under the Railways (Interoperability) Regulation 2011 as amended from the Safety Authority, namely the Office of Rail and Road, before putting into use a structural subsystem, namely the upgraded track subsystem at Stalybridge.

Improvement Notice issued to Chiltern Railways

  • Issue date: 19 February 2024
  • Compliance date: 30 May 2024
  • Status: Complied
  • Public register ID: I/240219-1-JGT

Chiltern Railways has failed to provide, despite numerous previous requests, suitable and sufficient evidence of operating compliance of the flexible plastic covers installed over the communication device in wheelchair areas.
